Welcome to Automotive Engineering Career Info

This site provides information for parents and students about automotive engineering opportunities after 10th class, including videos and images.

Career Path

  • After 10th - Choose Science stream with PCM.
  • After 12th - Appear for engineering entrance exams (JEE, state exams).
  • B.Tech/B.E in Mechanical/Automobile Engineering.
  • Specialize or pursue Masters/Research.
